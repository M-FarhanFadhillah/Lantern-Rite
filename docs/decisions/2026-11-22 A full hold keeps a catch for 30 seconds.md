# 2026-11-22 A full hold keeps a catch for 30 seconds
PRD section: §6 (Cargo grid).
Was: §6 gives no rule for a catch that doesn't fit the hold, and says rods, engines, lights and the ice box share the grid with fish.
Now: The user decided, for M1, that when a new catch doesn't fit the hold, the server keeps it pending for 30 seconds while the catch card waits. The player rearranges the hold or throws a fish overboard (discard). If there is still no room when the time runs out, the new fish is released. In M1 only fish take hold cells; gear joins the grid when boat equipment arrives.
Why: The Rowboat's 4×3 hold fills after a few catches (a large fish takes 6 of 12 cells). Without a way to swap, rowing out for rarer fish with a hold full of common fish is pointless until you sell.
