# 2026-11-22 M1 includes the Rowboat
PRD section: §16 (M1 node), with §6 for context.
Was: M1 was listed as "Harbor, 12 fish, track-bar reel, sell, save, cargo grid". No milestone scheduled boats, although the cargo grid is the boat's hold (§6), the core loop includes sailing out (§3), the tutorial ends with boarding a boat (§12), and M2's Reef and Marsh need boat tier 1.
Now: The user decided M1 includes the free Rowboat (tier 0, Harbor only) with simple piloting. Players spawn at the dock and can catch a first fish from the pier. They row out to Harbor spots where rarer fish bite, and the catch goes into the Rowboat's 4×3 hold (the cargo grid). Hull damage, boost, hazards, sinking and passengers stay out until M2 or later. Piloting follows §6: the driver's client owns the boat physics, and the server checks speed and position. PC uses W/S and A/D; mobile uses a thumbstick.
Why: M1's gate judges whether the first 10 minutes feel like this game, and sailing out is part of the core loop. Building the cargo grid on a real hull avoids reworking it later.
